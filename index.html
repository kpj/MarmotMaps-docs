Follow your own GPX routes on the trail.

Marmot Maps is a minimal hiking app for iPhone. Load your GPX files, see where you are on the trail, and hike - with no accounts, no social feeds, no subscriptions, and no clutter.

Bring Your Own GPX Routes

Point Marmot Maps at any folder in iOS Files or iCloud Drive to load all your tracks, routes, and waypoints at once, or open individual .gpx files from Files, Safari, or AirDrop.

Follow Routes on the Trail

See your live GPS position, compass heading, trail completion percentage, and remaining distance and time at a glance - plus optional haptic and notification Off-Trail Alerts if you wander off course with your phone in your pocket.

Interactive Elevation Profile

Tap or drag along the height chart to inspect exact distance, climb, and estimated walking time to any point on the route, with a synchronized marker on the map. Pinch to zoom into steep climbs.

Elevation Coloring & Topographic Maps

Keep classic solid-color trails or color routes by altitude. Switch anytime between OpenStreetMap, OpenTopoMap, Stadia Maps, Thunderforest, or your own custom HTTPS tile server.

Offline Caching & Power Save

Cache tiles for offline backcountry trips with supported tile providers, and reduce GPS and compass activity with Power Save Mode to stretch battery life on long hikes.

Private by Design

No sign-up, no cloud uploads, no analytics SDKs, and no ads. Your GPX files and location stay strictly on your iPhone.

Backcountry Safety Notice: Trail routes, elevation profiles, duration estimates, and GPS positions are provided for informational reference only and can be incomplete or inaccurate. Always carry backup navigation and exercise sound judgment outdoors.
