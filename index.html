iOS • Free • No Sign-Up • Zero Tracking

Your GPX trails, fast and offline in the backcountry.

Point Marmot Maps at any folder of GPX files in iOS Files or iCloud Drive. Inspect interactive elevation profiles, monitor live trail progress, and hike with zero clutter.

Folder-First GPX Library

Load an entire folder of GPX tracks and waypoints at once—or open individual .gpx files directly from Files, AirDrop, or Safari.

Interactive Elevation Profiles

Scrub and pinch-to-zoom elevation charts with synchronized map markers, elevation-colored trail gradients, and Naismith's Rule pace estimates.

Live On-Trail Status

See completion percentage, remaining distance and climb, compass heading, and off-trail distance warnings at a glance.

Offline Maps & Power Save

Cache tiles for offline mountain travel with Stadia Maps, Thunderforest, or custom HTTPS servers, and extend battery life with Power Save Mode.

Built-In Sample Trails

Explore Alpine sample trails right out of the box before importing your own routes.

100% Private by Design

No accounts, no analytics SDKs, no ads, and no cloud uploads. Your GPX routes and GPS location stay strictly on your iPhone.

Backcountry Safety Notice: Trail routes, elevation profiles, duration estimates, and GPS positions are provided for informational reference only and can be incomplete or inaccurate. Always carry backup navigation and exercise sound judgment outdoors.
